Clinical Capabilities

Specialized Emergency Solutions

From basic patient conveyance to mobile intensive care units, we operate round the clock to deliver hospital-grade care.

🚨

24/7 Emergency Dispatch

Rapid response teams mobilized instantly for cardiac arrests, trauma, strokes, and respiratory crises.

  • Sub-8 minute urban arrival
  • Pre-hospital telemetry to ER
  • Defibrillation & airway support
🏥

Inter-Hospital ICU Transfer

Designed for critically ill patients requiring continuous mechanical ventilation, infusion pumps, and doctor monitoring.

  • Bed-to-bed medical handover
  • Multi-parameter patient monitors
  • Certified emergency physician on board
👶

Neonatal & Pediatric Care

Dedicated transport incubators, specialized neonatal ventilators, and experienced pediatric emergency teams.

  • Temperature-controlled incubators
  • Pediatric-calibrated medication
  • Gentle suspension transit
✈️

Air Ambulance Repatriation

Domestic and international aeromedical evacuation with specialized fixed-wing and rotor aircraft.

  • Flight doctor & nurse pairing
  • Cabin pressure compensation
  • Global clearance handling
🏟️

Event Standby & Medical Cover

On-site medical squads, mobile first-aid stations, and ambulance standby for sports, concerts, and VIP events.

  • On-site triage stations
  • Crowd-ready paramedic carts
  • Direct liaison with local authorities
🦽

Non-Emergency Transport

Comfortable, wheelchair-accessible transport for dialyses, chemotherapy, and routine specialist consultations.

  • Hydraulic wheelchair lifts
  • Punctual scheduled pickups
  • Full companion assistance
