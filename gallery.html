Case Walkthrough

Behind the Sirens: Anatomy of a Code-3 Call

Follow the chronological minutes of an actual high-acuity cardiac emergency run from initial 911 dispatch to hospital cath-lab handoff.

00:00 • EMERGENCY CALL

911 Dispatch & GPS Triangulation

Central computer-aided dispatch receives an urgent call regarding chest pain and unconsciousness. The nearest active ALS unit is pinpointed and assigned automatically.

00:01 • ROLL-OUT

Vehicle En Route with Sirens Active

Paramedics hit the road in under 55 seconds. Automated traffic preemption signals clear green avenues along the quickest digital route.

00:06 • ON-SCENE

Immediate Assessment & 12-Lead ECG

Paramedics arrive at patient bedside with Zoll X Series monitor. A STEMI (acute myocardial infarction) is confirmed within 90 seconds of entry.

00:12 • TELEMETRY

Hospital Cath-Lab Telemetry Uplink

High-resolution 12-lead ECG waveforms transmitted via satellite directly to on-call cardiologist while aspirin, heparin, and oxygen are administered.

00:22 • ARRIVAL

Direct Bedside Trauma ER Handover

Ambulance rolls into the hospital emergency dock. The patient bypasses the ER waiting area and moves immediately onto the catheterization table.
