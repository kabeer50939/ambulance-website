World-Class Emergency Infrastructure

Behind every rapid response on the road lies a sophisticated ecosystem of state-of-the-art dispatch and maintenance facilities.

Equipped with computer-aided dispatch (CAD), dynamic traffic telemetry, and instant hospital triage connectivity.

Medical Decontamination Zone

UV-C sterilization and automated biohazard sanitization bays ensuring zero hospital-acquired infections.
